What to expect

Here is exactly what happens on your call.

01

Your provider reviews your case

They will have reviewed your intake before the call. No repeating yourself. They come in knowing your history, what you've tried, and what hasn't worked.

02

You get a root cause assessment

Your provider identifies what is actually driving your acne - gut, hormones, diet, inflammation. You will understand your skin better after this call than after years of treating the surface.

03

You leave with a plan

Whether you proceed or not, you leave with clarity - what is causing it, what would fix it, what the next step looks like.
